Balanced and unbalanced forces

Unit: Forces

Key learning points

  • {'keyLearningPoint': 'Two forces of equal size, acting in opposite directions on an object, are balanced.'}
  • {'keyLearningPoint': 'The resultant force is the sum of the forces acting on the object, taking into account their direction.'}
  • {'keyLearningPoint': 'When forces are balanced there is no resultant force and no change in speed.'}
  • {'keyLearningPoint': 'When there are two forces acting in opposite directions, the resultant force acts in the direction of the bigger force.'}
  • {'keyLearningPoint': 'The greater the resultant force on a given object, the greater or faster the change in speed.'}

Pupil lesson outcome

I can use the idea of resultant force to explain whether a change of speed occurs or not.
